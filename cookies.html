Cookie Policy

Official laboratory governance and editorial compliance guidelines for KitchenNotes.

1. Cookie Mechanics

Cookies are small alphanumeric files placed on your device to maintain theme choices (Cast Iron Dark vs. Parchment Light) and remember ratio calculator parameters across browser sessions.

2. Performance & Analytics Cookies

We utilize Google Analytics (Tag G-0LY0HY7L01) to evaluate which culinary notes and ratio tools are most helpful to our readership.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can adjust or disable cookie storage via your individual browser security settings at any time without degrading basic article readability.
